## Problem

QLU's outbound Dialer had to place high-volume recruitment calls — thousands per day — with reliability good enough that a dropped call was the exception, not the norm. On top of the Dialer, the new AI Voice SDR had to autonomously place calls, run a conversation, and log disposition without a human in the loop.

## Approach

Owned the Dialer's reliability layer and built the AI Voice SDR as a thin orchestration layer on top of it — so every autonomous call reuses the same call-state, webhook, and real-time-event infrastructure as a recruiter-driven call.

## What I Built

### Call Reliability & State

A custom Twilio Voice integration that handles connection pooling across Twilio accounts, automatic failover when an account throttles, and a call-state machine that tracks every leg of an outbound call — initiated, ringing, in-progress, completed, failed — with idempotent transitions.

### Status Webhooks

Twilio's status callbacks are processed and reconciled against the call-state machine. Webhook handlers are idempotent (Twilio retries aggressively) and write every state transition to PostgreSQL with a tamper-evident audit trail.

### Real-Time Call Events over WebSockets

Every state transition fans out over a WebSocket channel keyed per call and per recruiter. The dashboard re-renders the live call state in place — recruiter disposition controls, AI call transcripts, and post-call summaries — as the call progresses.

### AI Voice SDR

The AI Voice SDR is built directly on the Dialer: same call reliability, same webhooks, same WebSocket channel. The autonomous call path adds speech recognition on the inbound leg, a response-generation step conditioned on the candidate profile and the campaign brief, and a disposition step that writes the outcome back into the same call-state machine a human-driven call uses.

## Stack

NestJS · React · Twilio Voice · WebSockets · PostgreSQL · GCP

## Impact

The Dialer is the backbone QLU's AI Voice SDR is built on — so autonomous and human-driven calls share one reliability model. Together they handle thousands of concurrent outbound calls per day for recruitment outreach.
